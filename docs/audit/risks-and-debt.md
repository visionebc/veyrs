# Risk and technical-debt register

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Status after the 0.41.0 fix round (2026-11-17).** The owner asked for every item to be fixed; the Status column records what happened to each row. Items first written as "reported, not fixed" that remain so (TD-11, TD-12, TD-39, TD-59, TD-61) wait for an owner decision. The body text of each row still describes the behaviour found on 0.39.1.

**How to read it.** Severity: **High** means wrong results, a cross-tenant effect, data loss, or a broken shipped feature on a default path. **Medium** means a limited, conditional or operational impact. **Low** means hygiene. Effort is a rough size of the fix, not of its testing: **S** (hours), **M** (days), **L** (a week or more, or a design decision). Evidence is `path:line` at 0.39.1 (`ab8103e`); the backend is identical to 0.39.0 apart from the version string. "Read" means derived from reading the code and not reproduced at runtime, unless the row says otherwise (POST requests and test runs were out of bounds).

## 1. Security risks (owned by the security register)

The [security risk register](../security/risk-register.md) holds 28 security risks (R-01 to R-28: 2 High, 11 Medium, 15 Low) with full evidence and recommendations. They are not repeated here. Its High items:

| ID | Title | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Pinned dependencies carry known vulnerabilities (98 advisories in 10 of 54 packages, OSV query on 2026-11-17) | High | Fixed in 0.41.0 (all 10 packages upgraded, python-jose replaced by PyJWT; OSV re-query 2026-11-17: 0 advisories in 55 pins) |
| R-02 | Internal git history contains a plaintext administrator password and a database dump | High | Not a bug: verified 2026-11-17, history holds no var/backups or .bootstrap-credentials objects (0 of 135 commits); stale comment corrected |

The security-relevant items below (TD-03, TD-04, TD-23, TD-24, TD-45) were found by the backend and integration agents and are not in that register.
